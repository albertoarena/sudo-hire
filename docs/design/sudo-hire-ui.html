    Privacy

    Everything stays on your Mac. No account, no telemetry.

    Sudo Hire contacts
    • The job boards that you keep on, at most once an hour.
    • The career pages of the companies that you add, at most every 6 hours.
    • The European Central Bank, once a day, for exchange rates.
    Search words are fixed (php, laravel). Your keywords never leave your Mac.
    Stored on your Mac
    • Jobs, for 30 days. Saved jobs stay until you remove them.
    • Your settings and the companies that you add.
    • Exchange rates and a log file with warnings and errors.

    Troubleshooting

    Most problems come from one source, or from a filter that is too strict.

    We Work Remotely: certificate error at 12:05. Other sources are fine.
    No new jobs for days

    Check Posted within and your keywords in Jobs. In Where you work, a short time difference or "Hide" for Location not stated also removes many jobs.

    A source shows "Certificate error"

    The secure connection to the source failed. A network filter or an old certificate list can cause it. Update Sudo Hire. If it continues, report the problem.

    No notifications

    Open System Settings, Notifications, Sudo Hire, and allow notifications. Sudo Hire only notifies when new jobs match.

    "Stopped in the background"

    The part of the app that reads sources stopped. Choose Restart in the menu bar list. Your jobs and settings are safe.

    Report a problem
    The report has the app version, the macOS version, the PHP version and the status of each source. It has no settings, keywords or jobs.
